# Coparentalité Zen — promo 30 s · Mid-century Cartoon

Format 1080×1920 (9:16), 24 fps, ~32 s. French narration (calm female voice), burned-in captions + `.srt`.
Brand: navy #4E6381, coral #E4A196, sage #9AA791 on cream #FCF9F6, ink #101B2C. The user's official logo closes the film.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Onboarding card (3 steps)** — "Créez votre foyer → Invitez l'autre parent → Organisez-vous". Faithful to the site, but it sells set-up, not the relief.
2. **Before / after split** — the same week shown twice: left chaos (messages, forgotten receipts), right calm. Clear, but negative for half the film; the brand is "zen".
3. **One split that closes** — open on a frame split in two colour blocks (two homes), the child standing on the seam; three numbered features each stitch something across the seam (planning, dépenses, vacances); at the end the two blocks slide together into one ground and the child raises their arms — the exact pose of the logo's child — and the shot irises into the real logo.

**Chosen: 3.** It has one subject (the child between two homes), one turn (the seam closes), and an ending that echoes the opening. The features are the "how", which fits a scene style (an onboarding card for a service). Opening image and ending come from the brand itself (the logo's two parents + child), not from the style's demo.

## 2. Logline and arc

Between two homes a child has a thousand questions; Coparentalité Zen puts the whole family on one calendar — and the two halves of the frame become one.
Setup: split frame, two parents, question bubbles → Turn: a medallion stamps on the seam and opens the app → three steps, each crossing the seam → Ending: the blocks merge, child's arms up, match-cut into the logo, CTA held still.

## 3. Benchmark

- **1950s classroom "how-to" films** — learn: direct address, one sentence per picture, numbered cards. Don't take: any title design or narration text.
- **Charley Harper's mid-century prints** — learn: a flat geometric pattern that reads as decoration and information at once (our calendar → textile payoff). Don't take: any drawing.

## 6. Cue map

- 120 bpm, 4/4. Bars 1–2 (0–4 s): pizz walking bass + brushes, vibraphone comping, muted. Bar 2.25: stop (silence 4.5–5.0).
- 5–9: flute melody enters over vibes; snaps on 2 & 4.
- 9 / 15 / 20: each step has its own vibraphone chord (Fmaj9 / Dm9 / Gm9→C13), struck on the step's downbeat.
- 25–32: bass walks up a step (payoff), the three step chords return one per beat, final Fmaj9 tremolo held to the end.
